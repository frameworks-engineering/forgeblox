July 23, 2026: Welcome to FORGEBLOX! We are currently building the site, conducting in-depth research into the layouts of the old 2005 ROBLOX in an attempt to bring it back.

Since the FORGEBLOX website is currently under construction, why not submit an issue to our repository if you come across any bugs? It’s simple, easy, and free! Plus, FORGEBLOX is open source. You can take our code and republish it, modifying whatever you like! Just keep in mind: we use the MIT License, so be sure to read it carefully—or ask an AI to summarize it for you. Cheers, FORGEBLOXians! Thanks.

Welcome to FORGEBLOX! With your help, we have built a community of members with a passion for building, inventing and playing! Best of all, it's completely FREE! With FORGEBLOX, you can...

Invent!

If you can imagine it, you can build it (see how it works) on FORGEBLOX! Build vehicles, characters, buildings or whatever you want with the FORGEBLOX pieces.

Play FORGEBLOX!

Play lots of cool games, all built by the FORGEBLOX community. Find a game to match your interest. Compete for high score and FORGEBLOX points.

Build FORGEBLOX!

Build your own games to earn even more FORGEBLOX points. You can build puzzles, driving games, construction games – use your imagination and show your work to your friends!

Earn FORGEBLOX Points!

Everything you do on FORGEBLOX earns FORGEBLOX points. Publish a popular game or get high score to earn LOTS of points.
